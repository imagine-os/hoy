# Generated FAQ pricing contract

SW-02's W-10 pricing projection builds on the scoped Home pricing slice in PR106. [The shared contract](../../src/modules/website/publishedPricing.contract.ts) describes the published `plans` input, tenant scope, studio-date refresh, local events and pending backend mapping. [The FAQ contract](../../src/modules/website/faqPricing.contract.ts) adds `faq_entries` and the generated-table, search and JSON-LD views. The FAQ additionally declares `plans.active`, which its legacy fallback reads; Home does not acquire that unused dependency. These are content projections, with no saved table preferences.

Use one shared catalog reader for Home and FAQ. Preserve its request-generation/provider guards, observed-publication state, error handling, retry and subscription/timer cleanup. A failed or pending read must not restore stale legacy generated prices. Visible table prices, generated search text and structured data must use the same projection. The optional `frontend_catalog` and `frontend_publication` JSON fields are browser-only `CatalogPlan` extensions; their backend mapping remains pending.

The 16 seeded FAQ rows include two generated directives: `faq_s1_1` lists class/package/private families; `faq_s3_1` lists gifts. Three other authored answers contain seed-time product terms or amounts: `faq_s1_2`, `faq_s1_5` and `faq_s1_6`. This change does not rewrite those answers. PC06 real single/private amounts require owner review. Cancellation, freezing, illness, refunds, membership/affiliate benefits and gift policies remain separate. Customer C-14/C-15 currently render answer text directly; their raw pricing tokens are a separate scope, not repaired by a W-10 renderer override.

For every changed public pricing field, update shared/consumer contracts, page spec data, synthetic fixtures and tests together. Run `node scripts/test-public-home-pricing-contract.mjs` and `node scripts/test-faq-published-pricing-contract.mjs` for source/schema/spec/test-reference drift, then the relevant Home and FAQ domain, lifecycle and native publication checks. The drift checks inspect source bindings; they do not execute or certify runtime tests.

The generated projection and optional renderer design reuse the pricing scope of saved local commit `a07a9799ce287316646d8727fab615deccfbb3ad`. Its old duplicate reader and unrelated contact imports are not part of this contract. No real publication service, payment, email, canonical server clock, durable cross-device history or whole-frontend CMS readiness is claimed.
