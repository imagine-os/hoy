3. The provider must distinguish an **occupancy-neutral undo** from new admission: same existing booking ID/tenant/user/session, checked_in→booked, same mat and only the status+checked_in_at change. Preserve ledger/payment/purchased terms/mat/session identity, all other booking history fields and existing audit source/action/role semantics. Let the adapter derive occupancy as it already does; no manual count decrement or no-show policy rewrite. A valid full class retains its occupied seat. Missing/invalid/conflicting legacy mat/capacity history must not be silently assigned/repaired; any unprovable case is explicitly unverified pending root's concrete contract review.
5. Use a stable scoped nonce/fingerprint receipt containing IDs/hashes/allowlisted historical outcome, no names, notes, contacts or raw roster body. Fresh active actor/owned target/session checks are required inside the new transaction and around replay/postcommit acknowledgement; do not rely solely on the generic atomic callback-skipping replay path. Exact retry after time advances must not update checked_in_at again, add another undo audit or resurrect a row after a newer check-in.
