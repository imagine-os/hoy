## New and changed contracts
- `MarkdownViewer` props are **additive**: `directive?`, `figure?` and `headingIds?`. Every existing
  caller (`/#/docs`, the knowledgebase, A-06) keeps its behaviour; without `directive` a `{{…}}` line
  renders as a muted code chip and without `figure` a titled image is a plain `<img>`.
- `preprocessMarkdown()` keeps its signature and now applies three transforms: `[screenshot: …]` →
  `placeholder:` image (unchanged), `{{kind:arg}}` → fenced ```live block, and a GFM pipe table →
  fenced ```table block. `headingSlug()` is exported and is the single source of heading anchors.
- Ops-manual front matter has **six mandatory keys**: `title`, `role`, `part` (`I`…`VII`), `version`,
  `updated`, `summary`. A chapter without `part` still renders, in a trailing unnamed group.
- `manualIndex.ts` adds `PARTS`, `partOf()`, `chaptersByPart()`, `headingsIn()`, `readingTime()`,
  `searchChapters()`, `placeholdersIn()`, `START_HERE` and `LEGACY_SLUGS`; `Chapter` gains `part`,
  `summary`, `words` and `figures`.
- A directive is written **alone on its line**. A number M-08 or `pricing.ts` owns is never typed into
  a chapter — see `docs/rules/documentation.md` §7.
