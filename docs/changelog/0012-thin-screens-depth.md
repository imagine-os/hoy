## New and changed contracts
- **New tables (5).** `media_assets` (one art slot per place the app shows imagery), `payroll_runs`
  (a monthly teacher settlement), `payroll_lines` (a class, bonus or adjustment inside a run, with a
  per-teacher `paid_at` / `paid_method`), `legal_acceptances` (append-only proof of which version a
  member accepted). `legal_documents` is rewritten in place, and `content_articles` gains
  `publish_at`. 38 → 42 tables; every one carries its `rls` access contract and bilingual labels, so
  M-03 lists them and `npm run sql` emits the intent into `supabase/schema.sql` and
  `docs/data-model.md`.
- **`src/data/payrollCalc.ts`** is the only place payroll arithmetic lives: `monthPeriod`, `local`,
  `inPeriod`, `classLinesFor`, `runTotal`, `byTeacher`. No React, no provider — the seed imports it
  too. Anything that computes a teacher's pay calls it instead of multiplying again.
- **`wompiPayout()`** (`src/modules/admin/payouts.ts`) is the dispersion seam, documented at the top
  of the file the way `wompiCheckout()` is. Replacing its body is the whole of wiring real payouts.
- **`resolveLegalTokens(body, { tenant, policy })`** (exported from the `LegalDocument` organism)
  resolves `{{tenant.*}}` and `{{policy.*}}` in a legal body and renders an unknown token visibly as
  `⟨token⟩`, the way a missing i18n key renders. Legal copy never repeats a number a screen owns.
- **`MediaPlaceholder({ slotKey })`** — a component asks for a slot key, the media library answers.
  The keys are the contract: `class.hero`, `teacher.portrait`, `event.cover`, `studio.tour`,
  `site.hero`, `site.about`, `site.contact.map`.
- **`/app/legal/:kind`** is a customer route reusing the A-06 spec; C-19's legal rows now point in-app
  instead of out to the website, and the waiver is reachable from there.
- Two components join D-02: `LegalDocument` (organism) and `MarkdownEditor` (molecule), each with a
  meta, states and usages.
