# Hoy all roles, review round 1 fixes (Hoy frontend)

Prompt intent: fix what review round 1 found in the Hoy frontend on `hoy-all-roles` (M1–M5, L2–L8), test first; record L1 and L9 as known limits.

Decision:
- **Release order (M2).** `company-os.json` now requires `>=1.7.0`: this branch uses credits 1.2.0 (the studio-cancel lot), the Hoy recipe's `public_site` role and its `studio_private`, `hours_override`, `staff_note` and `privacy_request` policies, none of which v1.6.0 carries. `requires` cannot say the other half of the order (playbook E9), so: **before this frontend deploys, the Company OS release is deployed, the Hoy recipe is applied, the `public_site` service identity is created and `COMPANY_OS_READ_TOKEN` is set on the Hoy server.** Without them the signed-out site reads empty and `studio_private` would be written under the old policies.
- **The pay data move (M1)** is a scripted, idempotent operator step instead of "Administration saves Settings once": `scripts/move-private-settings.ts` moves `payroll`, `integrations` and the staff-only contacts from `studio_settings` to `studio_private` through the Company OS API, in one `/batch`, with the app's own split (`settingsSplit.ts`). Credentials only from the environment; dry run by default; it names what moves, never the values. Run it the day the release reaches an environment:
  - `COMPANY_OS_URL=https://<api> COMPANY_OS_TENANT=<slug> COMPANY_OS_PLATFORM_KEY=<key> node scripts/move-private-settings.ts` (dry run),
  - the same with `--apply` (writes),
  - the same with `--check` (exit 1 while pay data is left: the go-live gate).
  `COMPANY_OS_TOKEN=<platform token or an Administration session>` works in place of the platform key.
- **Holiday closed dates (M3).** The bookings `review_closed_dates` sync runs inside the override's save, awaited (no 1.5 s timer a closed tab or a sign-out could lose), and merges: dates set in Reception hours or the console stay; only the dates the written override covered before leave (unless another closed override still covers them). An edit that changes no closed date (a note, special hours) touches nothing. When the sync can't run, the override is still saved and the screen says so in words: `closed_dates_admin_only` (only Administration, the Super Admin, edits module settings) or `closed_dates_not_synced` (try again).
- **Holiday hours in production (M3, M5).** Settings › Hours in the production build showed only the reception panel, so nobody could write the `hours_override` rows the site and app read. It now shows the reception panel and, under it, the holiday and special-hours editor (no Google Business column: not connected at launch). The reception panel lists the closed holidays too, so an Administration save there carries the ones a coordinator added; it re-reads them after an override changes the closed days.
- **Coordinator holiday saves (M5).** Every failure is the code's copy (`failureCopy`), never the raw code: `forbidden` without the recipe grant; with the grant (Company OS round-1 lane), the holiday saves and the screen says the bookings closed days are Administration's. The holiday import goes item by item and names the date and reason it stopped at.
- **Route check (M4).** The stale PENDING `/admin/activity` entry is gone: the production build drops the review-only branch (`PRODUCTION_BUILD || cos`), so no `audit_log` read is left. The dashboard's recent-activity card reads the Company OS audit trail like `/admin/activity` (`readAuditPage`, newest ten; a refusal is said in words) and its OPEN entry is gone. `/teach` and `/admin/content` stay in the launch (owner, 2026-10-31, lane H plan, P-03b): their OPEN entries carry `approved` and name their wave, the check prints them "owner-approved", and an OPEN entry on a route added after the audit without an approval or a wave fails (`ADDED_ROUTES`).
- **Low:** L2 view-as of a role nobody holds shows an explicit empty state, and "my" reads alias nobody (`self()`, `myPerson`), never the super admin's own records; L4 `hours_override.closed` is closed only when `true`; L5 the inbox composer shows the refusal's copy (a WhatsApp log a role may not write); L6 a failed settings save stops the spinner and says why; L7 CI runs `test-companyos-provider`, `test-admin-activity(-ui)`, the offline part of `test-real-signup`, `test-website-signed-out-live` (with the Hoy server's own dependencies), `test-launch-website-ui/content`, and the new checks; L8 the view-as UI test saves Settings while viewing as Administration and sees it refused with the view-as copy.

**Known limits (no change):**
- **L1:** the Hoy server's signed-out strip of the private settings sections knows two answer shapes (`data.value`, `data[].data.value`); an aggregate or other projection on `setting` passes untouched. The recipe's `public_site` policy is the real guard (it never reads `studio_private`), and after the M1 move `studio_settings` holds no pay data.
- **L9:** the public-read rate limit is per IP (20/s, burst 120). Visitors behind one carrier-grade NAT address share it and could see `rate_limited` together at peak; watch the `public: true` 429s in the logs after launch.

Alternative rejected: keeping the browser timer and only surfacing its failure (a closed tab still loses the sync); having bookings derive closed dates from `hours_override` on the server (the better fix, a Company OS platform change: raised, not done here); keeping `/teach` and `/admin/content` out of the launch until green (the owner approved launching them with "available later" sections).
