# Hoy all roles, lane H2: the studio data behind the launch screens on Company OS

Prompt intent: close the route-connectivity gaps that need Company OS data (owner, 2026-10-31, "Add lane H now"): the pay data every role could read in the one settings row (decision F-01), holiday hours, the staff inbox and member notes, the account-deletion request, publishing legal versions, the emergency contact, and the signed-out site's settings and mat map.
Decision: the recipe (Company OS `deploy/recipes/hoy.json`) splits the settings into the public `studio_settings` record and `studio_private` (payroll, integrations, staff-only contacts: only Administration writes and Finance reads it) and adds the entities `hours_override` and `staff_note`, the forms `privacy_request`, a customer's own `policy_acceptance`, the emergency contact on the person, and public_site reads of the public settings, holiday hours and booked mats (class and mat only). In this app: the settings adapter reads both records in one query and writes only the part that changed (a legacy row's pay data moves on the next save; `settingsSplit.ts`); `hours_overrides` is an entity adapter and every override write re-syncs bookings `review_closed_dates` (`closedDates.ts`, Super Admin); `message_log` reads `comms_log` plus `staff_note` (a WhatsApp is logged `logged`, a note is a staff note, email refuses `email_not_connected`, read stamps aren't stored: no inbound messages, R-04); `deletion_requests` is the `privacy_request` submission; `legal_documents` stay in the build and publishing a version customers accept sets it in people `signup_consents` (`legal_document_in_build`, `legal_document_current`); `profiles.emergency_contact` is the person's two fields and sign-up sends them as person fields; signed out, `tenants`, `hours_overrides` and `bookings` are read live (`PUBLIC_TABLES`, server `PUBLIC_ENTITIES`). Route check: the OPEN entries `sink message_log`, `unknown hours_overrides`, `read_only legal_documents`, `signed_out tenants (empty)`, `signed_out bookings (empty)` and `unknown deletion_requests` are deleted; `signed_out deletion_requests (write)` (the website form, no session) is OPEN, unassigned; BY_DESIGN `signed_out tenants (write)` has its new reason.
Alternative rejected: a field list on the one settings row (a field list can't narrow inside a JSON value); comms_log with a note channel for staff notes (marketing and the coordinator read the log; a note is staff only, and comms has no such channel); keeping the emergency contact on the member facet (a write policy can't limit fields, so a customer could set their own price_audience).
