# Hoy all roles: Company OS test seed from the made-up sample

Prompt intent: on test-hoy a super admin views the studio as any role and sees data (Hoy all-roles Wave 1, lane B), so Company OS's test tenant needs Hoy's made-up sample.
Decision: `scripts/seed-companyos.ts` (`npm run seed:companyos`) builds the sample (`buildSeed()`) on a clock frozen at a fixed date (`--as-of`, default 2026-11-01 06:00 Bogotá) and writes Company OS `deploy/seeds/hoy.json`: a made-up person per sample user (one per role, 30 customers plus the demo customer) and per teacher, their role keys (`memberships`), member and coach facets, the classes two weeks back and three weeks on, the approved payments of the recipe's products with numbered receipts, a credit lot per pack or trial, and only the bookings the rules would accept (one a day, each paid from a lot or by its own single-class payment), with deterministic ids. The teachers' names are made up (the real ones are published on the site); addresses are `@demo.hoyos.test`, phones `+57 300 555 01xx`. Product ids are left out until the Company OS loader resolves `$ref` (`--recipe-refs` writes them by key).
Alternative rejected: hand-writing the seed in Company OS (it would drift from the app's sample); importing the sample's bookings as they are (a pack would go negative and a person would hold two classes a day, which the rules never allow; the import skips them).
Files: `scripts/seed-companyos.ts`, `scripts/test-seed-companyos.mjs` (`npm run test:seed-companyos`), `package.json`.
