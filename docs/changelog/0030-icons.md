rejected: (1) Drawing ~130 more in-house glyphs — weeks of drawing and review to reach the consistency lucide already has; every new screen would wait on a drawing. (2) An icon font (Material Symbols) — a font download, first-paint flash and ligature names that fail silently. (3) Importing lucide in pages directly — scatters the seam; a later swap becomes a repo-wide edit. (4) Filled/duotone glyphs for the active dock item — lucide paths are open strokes, so fills produce odd shapes on history/calendar glyphs; the pill + weight reads better and works for every name. (5) For the intention: hiding A-05 behind a feature flag — Justin asked to erase it from the experience, and a flag keeps the route, the C-01 card, the strings and the sign-up hop alive as dead weight that a switch can bring back by accident. (6) Dropping the `intentions` table or its seed rows — the schema is the history record and removing seed rows would shift the deterministic PRNG for every later table.
