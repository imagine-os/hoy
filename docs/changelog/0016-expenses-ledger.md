## New and changed contracts
- **New tables (2).** `expense_templates` — a recurring fixed cost with `cadence` (`biweekly` | `monthly`) and
  `anchor_day`; deactivate, never delete, once it has generated rows. `expenses` — one cost, `kind` `fixed`
  (generated, `template_id` set) or `variable` (typed, `template_id` null); `paid_on = null` means por pagar; a
  paid row is never deleted by the generator. 42 → **44 tables**, both with `rls` and bilingual labels.
- **`src/data/expenseCalc.ts`** is the only place expense arithmetic lives: `dueDatesFor`, `fixedExpensesFor`,
  `fixedKey`, `sumAmount`, `monthlyCost`. No React, no provider — the seed imports it too.
- **`src/modules/admin/expenses.ts`**: `FinanceRange` / `FINANCE_RANGES` / `sinceDay()` are the shared window M-09
  and M-09c both use; `useExpenses()`, `expenseTotals()`, `payrollInRange()` (runs whose period overlaps the range),
  `useGenerateFixed()` (idempotent, skip-existing, never deletes).
- **Permissions**: `expenses.read`, `expenses.write` (finance, admin, super_admin).
- **Audit actions**: `expense.create`, `expense.pay`, `expense.delete`, `expense.generate`,
  `expense.template.create`, `expense.template.toggle` — all `source: admin`.
- **Balance rule** (M-09): `approved payments in range − Σ payroll_runs.total for runs with period_start ≤ today
  and period_end ≥ since − Σ expenses.amount with incurred_on ≥ since`. Written once in the spec's logic, the
  card's body text and chapter 14 of the manual.
