# Maintenance and space

The room is the product. With 15 mats and 4 classes a day there are 4 setups daily; each follows this guide.

## 1. Cleaning standard
| When | What |
|---|---|
| Opening | Room floor mopped, mirrors, full bathrooms, reception, light scenting |
| Between classes (15 min) | Studio mats disinfected, props tidy, floor dry, bins, bathrooms checked |
| Closing | Everything from opening + towel/mat wash, rubbish out, ventilation |
| Weekly | Walls, air filters, vents, supplies store, inventory |

## 2. Room setup
1. 15 mats in the class type's layout (rows or circle, as M-02 states in the class description).
2. Props per mat: blocks ×2, strap, blanket; bolster available depending on class.
3. Temperature and humidity at the class value (hot classes preheat 45 min ahead).
4. Light and music per the day's movement (Enraíza, Fluye, Arde, Libera).

> DECISION NEEDED: target temperature per class type and exact preheat time.

## 3. Supplies
1. Minimum level per item in the store; when reached, reorder. List: disinfectant, towels, paper, soap, water, scents, filters.
2. Purchases approved by coordination; invoices to finance the same week.

## 4. Equipment checks
| Equipment | Frequency | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Climate / heating | Daily (reading) · monthly (technician) | Maintenance |
| Sound | Daily before the first class | Front desk |
| Lighting | Weekly | Maintenance |
| Extinguishers and first-aid kit | Monthly, dated label | Maintenance |
| Locks, exits | Daily at closing | Front desk |

## 5. Physical safety
1. Exits always clear; nothing stacked in front of doors.
2. Dry floor in the hot room before the group enters.
3. Any damage or risk reported to coordination the same day with a photo in the internal group.

## 6. Records
1. Opening, between-class and closing checklists are signed on paper or in the internal form; coordination reviews them weekly.
