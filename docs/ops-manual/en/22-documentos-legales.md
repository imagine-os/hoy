## 2. Versions and consent
1. Each document has a **version, a language and a publication date**. A published document is never
   edited: a new version is published.
2. What the person accepted is stored with the exact version, the time and who recorded it. That is
   what counts in a dispute — not the current text.
3. When registering at the desk (S-04), the person accepts verbally and front desk records it: stored
   with the time, your name and the version (`10`).

{{table:consents}}

## 3. When a document changes
1. The new version is published in both languages on the same day.
2. Existing account holders are asked to accept again only if the change affects their rights or the
   price; if it is cosmetic, they are not.
3. The notice goes out through the channels in chapter `13`, never during quiet hours.

## 4. Contracts that are not in the app
Space rentals (`12`), teacher contracts (`16`) and suppliers live outside the system. What does live in
the system is the M-06 note with the amount, the conditions and the date.

## 5. What is simulated today
1. The documents exist, are linkable and are versioned, but **the body copy is provisional** until
   counsel delivers the wording.
2. There is no legal screen inside the member app yet: the links go to the site pages.
