# Front desk

Your home screen is **S-02 Front desk check-in**. From there you search members, see the Now / Next / Later strip, today's teachers and the desk actions. Every action is logged with your name.

[screenshot: S-02 — door board with search, day strip, teachers and actions]

## 1. Opening (45 min before the first class)
1. Turn on lights, climate and reception music; check the room is set up per `07`.
2. Sign in to HoyOS with your user → **S-01 Role home** → Front desk.
3. In **S-02** confirm the day's 4 classes, capacity (max 15) and that each teacher shows as "Expected".
4. Review each class's waitlist and yesterday's pending payments (unconfirmed transfers).
5. Read overnight WhatsApp messages (they arrived after 9pm; replies go out from 7am).
6. Count the cash float and write it on the closing sheet.

## 2. Greeting
1. Look at the person, smile, name if you know it: "Hola, Camila. Here for the 7?"
2. New person: "Hola, welcome to HOY. First time? I'll register you in a minute."
3. Before class, one useful question only: "Do you need a mat or did you bring yours?"

## 3. Check-in
1. In **S-02** pick the class in the selector; the list shows Expected, Checked in and Waitlist.
2. Search by name, phone, email or ID; tap the person → **Checked in**.
3. If it says "already checked in", do not duplicate: same person, or the teacher already marked attendance.
4. Discreet health marker: do not say it aloud; the teacher sees it on the roster.
5. At T+5 min after class start, anyone not arrived is a no-show: confirm it in S-02 so the waitlist promotes.

**Steps in HoyOS:** S-02 Front desk check-in → class selector → Find a member → Checked in. Cancel or move: action "Cancel or move a booking" (opens C-08b on the member's behalf).

## 4. Walk-ins and Bienvenida passes
| Pass | Price (COP) | Validity |
|---|---|---|
| Trial Class | $39.000 | 1 class, first time only |
| Single Pass | $58.000 | 1 visit |
| 3-Class Pack | $110.000 | 1 month |
| 10-Class Pack | $490.000 | see decision needed |

> DECISION NEEDED: 10-Class Pack validity (the brief says 1 month; P-01 Plans & prices says 3 months).

1. No room in the class they want: offer the next class of the day or the waitlist. One person, one class a day.
2. Prices are read in S-04 from the value model; never type them or negotiate them.

## 5. Register and take payment
1. **S-04 Register & take payment**, section **Who**: New / Existing. New: name, WhatsApp, email, emergency contact, birthday and consent (the person agrees verbally; you record it: it is stored with time, your name and policy version).
2. Section **What**: pick the Bienvenida pass or plan.
3. Section **How they pay**:
   - Wompi (link): send the link by WhatsApp; the order stays pending until the gateway confirms.
   - Card terminal (datáfono): charge; the order stays pending until confirmation.
   - Cash: settles immediately; put the money in the till and hand over the receipt.
   - Transfer / Nequi: ask to see the proof on screen, leave the order pending and note it for finance.
4. **Complete and check in**: the sale auto-checks the person into the class they bought. Receipt by WhatsApp and email.
5. Payment declined: the order stays pending with the reason; offer another method, do not blindly retry.

**Steps in HoyOS:** S-04 → Who → What → How they pay → Complete and check in. Look up later: M-06 CRM → Payments tab.

## 6. Memberships and pauses
1. Monthly Plan $520.000/month · Annual Plan $4.990.000/year. Only two options; no hidden discounts.
2. A member can freeze up to 30 days from **C-22 Manage membership**; if asked at the desk, do it from M-06 and leave a note.
3. They always get notice before every charge. If asked "when am I charged?", the date is in M-06 → Payments tab.
4. Cancel: access continues until the end of the paid period. No mazes: do not push back, record the reason.

> DECISION NEEDED: notice days for a pause (C-22 proposes 15 days notice; the brief does not fix it).

## 7. Cancellations, late arrivals and no-shows
| Situation | Rule | What you say |
|---|---|---|
| Cancels > 2 h before | Credit returns immediately | "Done, your credit is already back." |
| Cancels < 2 h before | Credit is spent | "Since it's under 2 hours, this class counts. Shall I move you to another one today?" |
| Move | Cancel + rebook in one action; no fee outside the window | "I'll move you to the 9:30, same credit." |
| Arrives late | Grace per M-08 | "Come in quietly; the teacher has started." |
| No-show | Confirmed in S-02 at T+5; credit is spent | WhatsApp template goes out, no scolding |

> DECISION NEEDED: late grace minutes and no-show fee ("Late check-in grace" and "No-show fee" fields in M-08).

## 8. Waitlist
1. Strict promotion by position; a released spot is offered for 30 minutes by WhatsApp (ignores quiet hours), then passes to the next person.
2. You may skip the order only with a logged reason (e.g. the person is already at the door).
**Steps in HoyOS:** S-02 → Waitlist section → Promote (reason). Member view: C-20.

## 9. Lost and found
1. Label with date, class and description; keep in the lost-and-found box; note in M-06 if you know whose it is.
2. Kept 30 days, then donated. Say so when handing items back.

## 10. Incidents
1. The person's safety first (`09`). Then record: note in **M-06** with category, time and what you did; tell coordination the same day.

## 11. End-of-day cash close
1. After the last class, count cash; compare with today's "Settled cash payment" entries in **M-07** filtered by your name.
2. List pending transfers with proof and send them to finance.
3. Lock cash in the safe, sign the sheet, switch off equipment and close using the `07` checklist.
**Steps in HoyOS:** M-07 All activity log → filter today + source front desk → Export CSV.
