# Training checklists

Every new person goes through three stages —Day 1, Week 1 and Month 1— and signs off with whoever trains them. To
practise without touching real data, use your role's demo mode on the Home screen.

{{audience:09-checklists-de-entrenamiento}}

The numbers are chapters of this manual: 01 Who we are, 04 Front desk, 06 Teachers, 08 Incidents, 14 Payments and
the till, 18 Web and social, 20 Voice and tone, 24 Roles.

> IN HOYOS: S-01 Home → the role's demo mode.

## 1. Front desk
{{editable:coordinator}}

{{training:front_desk}}

| Stage | What you should have done |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Read 01, 04, 08 and 20 · have your own user · walk the space · practise check-in in demo mode · learn the greeting |
| Week 1 | Open and close with someone beside you · 5 real sales with each payment method · one cancellation inside and one outside the window · promote someone from the waitlist · close the till with finance |
| Month 1 | A full shift on your own · WhatsApp replies reviewed by coordination · read 07 and 13 · two weeks with no till differences |

## 2. Teachers
{{editable:coordinator}}

{{training:teacher}}

## 7. Marketing
{{editable:owner}}

{{training:marketing}}

Marketing is a new role at HOY. It looks after content, social media, the brand and campaigns. The **marketing kit**
(logos, templates and approved photos in one place) is coming soon to the hub; until then, work from chapters 18,
19 and 20.

| Stage | What you should have done |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Read 01, 03, 18, 19 and 20 · walk the studio and the website · get to know the brand manual |
| Week 1 | Propose the month's posting calendar · check which photos are approved · write 3 posts and have coordination review the tone |
| Month 1 | A month posted to the calendar · nothing posted that doesn't exist in the system (prices, times, promotions) · a first report to the owner |

## 8. Developer
{{editable:owner}}

{{training:developer}}

Developer is a new role at HOY. It looks after the developer tools, the documentation, each screen's specs and the
integrations.

| Stage | What you should have done |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Read 24, 25 and 26 · read the software documentation · run the app locally and turn on developer mode |
| Week 1 | Go through each screen's spec · understand what is simulated and what is real · one small change recorded in the documentation |
| Month 1 | One integration moved forward (with its Integrations card up to date) · the documentation and this manual updated in the same change |
