# Training checklists

Every new person follows Day 1 / Week 1 / Month 1 and signs off with their trainer. HoyOS has a demo
mode per role in **S-01 Role home**: use it to practise without touching real data.

The chapter numbers below are this manual's: `01` Who we are, `04` Front desk, `06` Teachers,
`08` Incidents, `14` Payments and the till, `20` Voice and tone, `24` Roles.

## 1. Front desk
| Stage | Checklist |
|---|---|
| Day 1 | Read `01`, `04`, `08`, `20` · own HoyOS user · tour of the space · practise check-in in S-02 (demo) · learn the greeting |
| Week 1 | Supervised opening and closing · 5 real registrations in S-04 with every payment method · handle a cancellation inside and outside the window · promote a waitlist · cash close with finance |
| Month 1 | Full shift alone · WhatsApp replies with tone reviewed by coordination · read `13` and `07` · no cash differences for 2 weeks |
