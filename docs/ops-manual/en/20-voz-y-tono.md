# Voice and tone

How HOY sounds is part of the product, just like the temperature of the room. This chapter applies to
everything: the door, WhatsApp, email, the site, social and the screens themselves.

## 1. Four words
If a message does not meet all four, it gets rewritten.

| Trait | Means | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human | We speak as people, not as a system | "We put your credit back." | "Your credit has been reinstated." |
| Close | Always informal, by name | "Hola, Mariana." | "Dear user." |
| Direct | One idea per sentence; the fact first | "Your 7:00 class was cancelled." | "We regret to inform you that…" |
| Present | We talk about today and what comes next | "There's a spot at 9:30, shall I hold it?" | "There may be availability in future." |

## 2. How we greet and speak
1. In person: eye contact, the name if we know it, one useful question. "Hola, Camila. Here for the 7?"
2. On WhatsApp: first line with the name and the fact; second line with the action. One emoji at most
   (🌿 is ours). No sustained capitals.
3. Always "tú" in Spanish. Never "usted", not in complaints and not with older people; if someone asks
   for "usted", respect it with that person.
4. No internal jargon in front of the customer: we say "credit", "booking", "membership"; we do not say
   "entitlement", "order", "flag".
5. Spanish first. English when the person prefers it, in the same tone.

## 3. How we say no
A "no" is said in full and with the way out.

| Situation | What we say |
|---|---|
| Class full | "That class is full. I'll put you on the waitlist and we'll message you on WhatsApp if a spot opens." |
| Cancelled outside the window | "Since we're inside the window, this class counts. Shall I move you to another one today?" |
| Already took today's class | "You've done yours today. Shall I book you tomorrow at the same time?" |
| Wants a discount that doesn't exist | "We don't discount outside the plans, but the annual works out much better per month. Want me to show you?" |
| Asks for something policy doesn't allow | "That one isn't mine to do. I'll pass it to coordination today and they'll get back to you." |

## 4. What we never write
1. We do not blame the person ("you didn't cancel in time").
2. We do not promise what policy does not allow; the current value is in M-08, not in memory.
3. We do not send non-urgent messages during quiet hours.
4. We do not comment on anyone's body, weight or level.
5. We do not use the word "customer" when talking to the person: it's "member", or better, their name.
6. We do not write health data, photos of payment proofs, or another person's information (`23`).

## 5. The voice inside the system
The app and site copy exist in ES and EN, with Spanish as the source. If a screen sounds wrong, that is
a copy change, not a complaint: report it to coordination with the screen code and the exact text.

## 6. The name
"HOY" is written in capitals when it is the brand; the wordmark is lowercase and is an image, not text
(`19`). The line that sums us up, and that can close a message when it fits: **la vida es HOY.**
