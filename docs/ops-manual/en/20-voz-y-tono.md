# Voice and tone

How HOY sounds is part of the experience, just like the temperature of the room. This applies to everything: the
door, WhatsApp, email, the website, social media and the system's screens.

{{audience:20-voz-y-tono}}

## 1. Where our voice comes from
Everything in this chapter comes from the 2026 brand manual. The Spanish is the original; the English below is a
written translation.

{{source:manual-de-marca}}

**Manifesto**

> Between what was and what has not yet arrived, there is this moment. A space to stop, breathe, move, feel and come
> back to yourself. We are not looking to escape routine, but to learn to inhabit it differently. With more presence.
> More awareness. More connection. HOY is movement, and it is also pause. It is energy and balance. It is body, mind
> and experience. You do not always have to go faster, do more or get further. Sometimes you simply have to come
> back.

**Purpose.** To make movement and breath a way back to ourselves and into the present. Because everything starts
HOY.

**Mission.** To create experiences that turn wellbeing into a way of living: more aware, present and connected.
Through movement, breath and community, HOY wants to become the place people choose to fold naturally into their
day, to move, to feel good and to enjoy the now.

## 2. Four keywords
**Present · Movement · Connection · Experience.** If a message, a post or a screen touches none of the four, it
probably doesn't sound like HOY.

## 3. Five traits
| Trait | What it means | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|---|
| Present | Lives the moment with attention, neither running ahead nor falling behind | "There's room at 9:30, shall I hold it?" | "Availability may arise in the future." |
| Human | Every person is different; what is real matters more than what is perfect | "We've given your credit back." | "Your credit has been reinstated." |
| Close | Natural, simple and direct, with no speeches or pretension | "Hi, Mariana. Your 7:00 class was cancelled." | "Dear user, we regret to inform you that…" |
| Sensory | Cares for the details; every interaction is pleasant and memorable | "We've left water and a towel for you at the door." | "Participants are advised to bring equipment." |
| Contemporary | Current, aesthetic and fresh, without wellness's usual codes | "Come and move for a while." | "Transform your body in 30 days." |

If a message doesn't pass all five traits, rewrite it.

## 4. How we greet and speak
{{editable:owner}}

1. **In person:** look at the person, use their name if you know it and ask one useful question. "Hi, Camila. Here
   for the 7 o'clock?"
2. **On WhatsApp:** the name and the fact on the first line; the action on the second. One emoji at most (🌿 is
   ours). No shouting in capitals.
3. **Always informal** (in Spanish, "tú"). If someone asks to be addressed formally, respect it with that person.
4. **No internal words** in front of customers: we say "credit", "booking", "membership".
5. **Spanish first.** English when the person prefers it, in the same tone.

## 5. How we say no
{{editable:owner}}

A "no" is said in full and with a way out.

| Situation | What we say |
|---|---|
| The class is full | "That class is full. I'll put you on the waitlist and we'll WhatsApp you if a spot opens up." |
| Cancelled outside the window | "It's too close to the class now, so this one counts. Shall I move you to another one today?" |
| Already had their class today | "You've done yours today. Shall I book you tomorrow at the same time?" |
| Asks for a discount that doesn't exist | "We don't do discounts outside the plans, but the annual one works out much better per month. Want me to show you?" |
| Asks for something the policy doesn't allow | "That's not something I can do. I'll pass it to coordination today and they'll get back to you." |

## 6. What we never write
1. We never blame the person ("you didn't cancel in time").
2. We never promise what the policy doesn't allow.
3. We never send non-urgent messages during quiet hours.
4. We never comment on anyone's body, weight or level. We don't talk about "levels" or "advanced classes": every
   class adapts to whoever arrives.
5. In front of the person we don't say "customer": we say "member", or better, their name.
6. We never write health details, photos of payment proofs or anyone else's information (see
   [Personal data](23-habeas-data.md)).

## 7. The voice in the system
The app's and the website's texts exist in Spanish and English; Spanish is the source. If a screen sounds wrong,
that is a copy change, not a complaint: tell coordination which screen it is and the exact text.

## 8. The name and the line
1. "HOY" is written in capitals when it is the brand. The logo is the handwritten word "hoy" with HUMAN CLUB, and it
   is an image, not text (see [Media](19-medios-y-artwork.md)).
2. **Human club** is the descriptor that goes with the name.
3. The line that sums us up, which can close a message when it fits: **"Everything that was is already behind you.
   Everything to come is not here yet. This is HOY."**
