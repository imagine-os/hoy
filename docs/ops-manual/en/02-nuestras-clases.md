# Our classes

HOY has seven classes: Ligereza, Híbrido, Fuego, Sólido, Centro, Alineación and Pulso. They are seven
methodologies that combine Pilates, Yoga, Barre, mobility, strength, cardio and breathwork, at different levels
of intensity. This chapter tells you what each one is and what to say when someone asks.

{{audience:02-nuestras-clases}}

## 1. How we talk about the classes
This is how the website puts it:

> We are not here to train bodies. We are here to awaken presence. We have seven classes, and each one is a
> different path. Some give you a base and strength. Others light you up. Others centre you, make you feel every
> detail, help you let go or understand how your body works. They all arrive at the same place: here, now.

1. In front of a customer we always use **the class name**: "the 7 o'clock Fuego", "Centro at 9:30". The names
   stay in Spanish in both languages.
2. The classes are challenging and done with the right alignment. The intensity changes from one to another;
   none of them is "for advanced people".
3. Nobody has to pick "the right one" on day one. They can try them all, stick to one or alternate.
4. HOY provides the mat and all the props. The person can bring a small towel and their bottle.

**What to say if they don't know which to choose:** "If you want to sweat, Fuego. If you want strength, Sólido.
If you want to let go or rest, Ligereza or Centro. They are all fine to start with."

## 2. Ligereza — I let go of what I don't need
> Release. Opening. Flow. Conscious movement.

1. **Method:** mobility, stretching and yin yoga. **Low intensity.** It closes with a 3 to 4 minute savasana.
2. It is for letting go of stored tension and leaving lighter.
3. Guided by Sara Crismatt Duque.

**What to say at the door:** "It's a gentle class, mobility and stretching. You leave lighter."

## 3. Híbrido — I am strong and flexible
> Fusion. Wholeness. Balance. The best of both worlds.

1. **Method:** Pilates and dynamic yoga. **Medium intensity.**
2. It brings strength and flexibility together in one class.
3. Guided by Raghu.

**What to say at the door:** "It's half Pilates, half dynamic yoga. You work strength and flexibility at once."

## 4. Fuego — light up your energy
> Ignition. Awakening. Life energy set free.

1. **Method:** dynamic Pilates and rumba. **High intensity, no rest.**
2. It is the cardio class: the rhythm of the music carries it.
3. Guided by Sara Estrada.

**What to say at the door:** "It's the most intense one: dynamic Pilates with rumba, no breaks. Bring water."

## 5. Sólido — your base is built from within
> Rooting. Building. Solidity from within.

1. **Method:** Pilates with weights and resistance. Full body. **Medium-high intensity.**
2. It builds strength from the centre, with control and precision.
3. Guided by Andre Cardona.

**What to say at the door:** "It's Pilates with weights. You work the whole body with control; no experience
needed."

## 6. Centro — I am here, now
> Presence. Anchoring. Deep silence. Back to yourself.

1. **Method:** meditation, conscious breathing and sound healing. It closes with a deep savasana.
2. It is the class of the pause: there is no rush here.
3. Guided by Tatiana Ramirez.

**What to say at the door:** "It's guided. You don't need to know how to meditate: just lie down, breathe and
follow the voice."

## 7. Alineación — I understand how I work
> Intelligence. Function. Clear biomechanics. Intelligent movement.

1. **Method:** functional dynamic yoga and vinyasas. **Medium intensity.** A 3 to 4 minute savasana.
2. It teaches you to move with purpose and to understand your own biomechanics.
3. Guided by María Camila Pinzón.

**What to say at the door:** "It's dynamic yoga with a lot of attention to alignment. You'll understand how your
body moves."

## 8. Pulso — I feel every fibre of my being
> Sensitivity. Micro-movements. Deep muscle intelligence.

1. **Method:** Barre, isometrics and pulses. **Medium intensity, total focus.**
2. The magic is in the small things: short, precise movements to the rhythm of the music.
3. Guided by Carolina Cifuentes.

**What to say at the door:** "It's Barre with small, held movements. No jumping, but you feel it in every
muscle."
