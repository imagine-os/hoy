# Our classes

At HOY you'll find several ways to move and to be under one roof: hot yoga, barre, pilates, meditation
and breathwork. They are not isolated disciplines or categories with levels and labels: they are
different paths to the same place — your body, your breath, your present. Each has its own rhythm, but
they share one intention: helping you step out of autopilot and feel yourself in your body again.

Nobody has to pick "the right one" on day one. You can try them all, stay with whichever suits you, or
alternate depending on how the week feels. All we ask is that you arrive wanting to be present; the
rest we build together, class by class.

## 2. Hot Yoga
Something happens when the body moves in heat: the mind stops resisting and starts to give. At HOY hot
yoga is practised in a room where the temperature rises on purpose — not to punish, but to help you get
faster to the place where the body opens, the muscles answer differently and the breath becomes the
centre of everything. It is not a class for sweating more: it is a class for feeling more.

Heat changes how you move. Postures that feel rigid when cold feel possible when warm. The body gets
more flexible, circulation picks up, and with each deep breath you let go of what you were carrying
before you walked in: the day's tension, the noise in your head, the city's hurry.

You need no previous experience. The heat can sound intimidating at first, but our teachers show you
how to move with it rather than against it: when to lower the intensity, when to drink, when to simply
stay still for a moment in child's pose. Every body finds its own rhythm in the same room, and that is
part of the practice too: learning to listen to yourself instead of comparing.

Over time, hot yoga becomes less about what you achieve on the mat and more about what you carry off
it: more clarity, more strength, more capacity to be present even when things get intense.

**What to say at the door:** "It's a heated room. Bring water and a towel; if it ever gets too much,
you just stay still for a moment and that's it. Nobody is going to look at you."

## 3. Barre
Barre at HOY combines the best of three worlds: the precision of pilates, the elegance of ballet and
the energy of functional training. The result is a high-intensity, low-impact class that works every
major muscle group without a single jarring jump or joint impact. It is demanding, and it feels kind to
your body.

What makes barre different is not the size of the movement but its precision. We work with
micro-movements, small controlled repetitions held just long enough for the muscle to shake before it
releases. The music sets the pace of each set, and that steady pulse is what gets you to the last ten
reps that seemed impossible at the start.

Our barre teachers train specifically for this discipline, because the quality of a class shows in the
details: the correction that lands just in time, the posture adjustment before anyone gets hurt,
knowing when to push a little more and when to let someone rest. It is not a generic toning class with
music in the background: it is an experience designed with intention.

**What to say at the door:** "Low impact, but intense. If it's your first time the teacher will adjust
your posture; you can ask them not to touch you and they'll respect it."

## 4. Pilates
Pilates starts in one place: the centre. That is where the strength is switched on that later holds
every movement, every posture, every gesture of the whole body. This discipline teaches you to move
with more control, more awareness and more precision — not through endless repetitions, but through
intention in every gesture.

It looks slow and lands deep: it strengthens the core, improves posture and teaches you to align a
body that, day to day, gets used to hunching over a screen or carrying weight without noticing. Each
exercise feels simple at first and asks more than it looks.

Our pilates teachers work from the detail: the position of the spine, the breath that accompanies each
movement, the small adjustment that turns an exercise from mechanical into effective. It doesn't matter
whether someone has never practised or has done it for years: the class adapts to their body and their
pace, with no comparisons and no pressure to reach a particular level.

What begins as strength in the centre of the body ends up showing in everything else: in how you walk,
how you sit, how you respond when something takes you by surprise.

## 5. Meditation
In the middle of constant noise, meditating is an almost radical act: stopping, even for a few minutes,
with no need to produce, reply or solve anything. At HOY we understand meditation as exactly that — a
space of stillness where the mind is allowed to simply be, without demanding absolute silence or a
perfect version of calm.

We include it because wellbeing does not live only in the body's movement: it lives in the pause too.
Practising regularly helps reduce accumulated stress, improves mental clarity and trains a capacity
that gets scarcer all the time — being present.

## 6. Breathwork
There is something curious about breathing: we do it non-stop and almost never notice it. At HOY we
invite you to do just that — notice it — and discover that in something so simple there is more calm
and more energy than you'd imagine.

It isn't about mastering a complex technique, but about reconnecting with something you already know
how to do. That's why it also lives inside our guided classes: a short, effortless pause that stays
with you long after you leave the studio.

## 7. The four movements
The system schedules by **movement**, not by discipline: Enraíza, Fluye, Arde and Libera. They are the
answer to "how do you want to feel today?", and there are four of them because there are four classes a
day. Each modality is assigned to a movement in M-02; the colour in the schedule and the app comes from
there.

| Movement | Feels like | Usually |
|---|---|---|
| Enraíza | grounding, holding, landing | pilates, breathwork |
| Fluye | releasing, linking, long breath | soft yoga, mobility |
| Arde | pulse up, shaking, sweating | barre, hot yoga |
| Libera | loosening, staying still, closing | meditation, restorative |
