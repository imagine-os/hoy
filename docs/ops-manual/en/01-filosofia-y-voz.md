# Philosophy and voice

## 1. What HOY is
1. A wellness club that is small on purpose: 15 mats, 4 classes a day, one class per person per day. Scarcity is part of the care, not a limitation we apologise for.
2. The product's central question is "¿Cómo quieres sentirte hoy?" — how do you want to feel today (screen A-05). Everything we do in the studio answers that question: the person arrives wanting to feel a certain way, and we make the path easy.
3. Conscious controls we promise and keep: membership pause of up to 30 days, leave without mazes, a kind cancellation window (2 hours), notice before every charge.

## 2. Brand voice
Four words. If a message does not meet all four, it gets rewritten.

| Trait | Means | Yes | No |
|---|---|---|---|
| Human | We speak as people, not as a system | "We put your credit back." | "Your credit has been reinstated." |
| Close | Always informal "tú", by name | "Hola, Mariana." | "Dear user." |
| Direct | One idea per sentence; the fact first | "Your 7:00 class was cancelled." | "We regret to inform you that…" |
| Present | We talk about today and what comes next | "There is a spot at 9:30, shall I hold it?" | "There may be availability in future." |

## 3. How we greet and speak
1. In person: eye contact, name if we know it, one useful question. "Hola, Camila. Here for the 7?"
2. On WhatsApp: first line with name and the fact; second line with the action. One emoji at most (🌿 is ours). No sustained capitals.
3. Always "tú" in Spanish. Never "usted", not in complaints nor with older people; if someone asks for "usted", respect it with that person.
4. A "no" is said in full and with the way out: "That class is full. I'll put you on the waitlist and we'll message you on WhatsApp if a spot opens."
5. No internal jargon in front of the customer: we say "credit", "booking", "membership"; we do not say "entitlement", "order", "flag".
6. Spanish first. English when the person prefers it, same tone.

## 4. What we never do
1. We do not blame the person ("you didn't cancel in time"). We explain the rule and what they can still do.
2. We do not promise what policy does not allow; the current value is in M-08.
3. We do not send non-urgent messages between 9pm and 7am.
4. We do not comment on anyone's body, weight or level.
