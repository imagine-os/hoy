# W-07 — Site · Classes

**Route** `/#/site/classes` · **Roles** super_admin, admin, coordinator, front_desk, finance, teacher, maintenance, customer, public · **Surface** public · **Spec** `spec.code === 'W-07'` (see `/#/dev/specs`)

## Purpose
The "Nuestras clases" introduction and one rich block per class — hot yoga, barre, pilates, meditación, respiración — each with its eyebrow, summary, opening paragraph, modality-name chip, duration from the matching modality and a 16:9 photo slot. The blocks alternate left/right on desktop and stack on mobile.

## Logic and integrations
- **Wordmark (0033)**: the closing panel h2 "Todo empieza HOY." / "Everything starts HOY." sets HOY as the inline wordmark in the heading colour (tone `current`: cream on the deep-blue panel in both themes). The PageHead eyebrow "Cómo te mueves en HOY" and the intro paragraphs stay text. Captures: `es-1280.jpg`, `es-1280-dark.jpg`, `es-390.jpg` (+ EN).
- Cards are generated from `brand.classOrder`; adding a class means adding one entry to `src/tenant/brand.ts`.
- Each block links to `/site/classes/:slug` and carries its modality's class tone from D-01 `classTones`.
- The trial price in the closing band is read from `src/tenant/pricing.ts` (`trial`).
- Integrations: none

## States

## Real vs mock
- Real: the intro and every class essay are the owner's copy from `src/tenant/brand.ts`; the facts
  (duration, intensity, heated) are joined live from the `modalities` table through
  `brand.classes[slug].modalitySlugs`; the trial price comes from `src/tenant/pricing.ts`.
- Mock / pending: the photography is pending — each class books a 16:9 `MediaSlot` with its art brief
  (see the shot list in `docs/website-vision.md`). Data is the browser-local `MockProvider` seed.
