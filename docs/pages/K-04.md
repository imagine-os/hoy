# K-04 — Decisions pending

**Route** `/#/manual/decisions` · **Roles** super_admin, admin, coordinator, front_desk, finance, teacher, maintenance, customer, public · **Surface** docs · **Spec** `spec.code === 'K-04'` (see `/#/dev/specs`)

## Purpose
Automatic list of everything the studio has not defined, extracted from the manual's "DECISION NEEDED" blocks, grouped by chapter and section.

Nothing on this page is written here: every item is a one-line `> DECISIÓN PENDIENTE:` /
`> DECISION NEEDED:` blockquote inside a chapter of K-03, and the page is the extraction. Answering one
means editing that chapter — the item then disappears from this list and from `ROADMAP.md` §E, which
reads the same source. Since 0.6.0 each chapter group carries its **part** eyebrow (I…VII), so a
decision can be read as "a Part IV money question" rather than as a flat list, and the running number
spans the whole manual so an item can be cited as "K-04 #07".

The 0.6.0 re-categorisation kept all 17 pre-existing flags and added 10 the new content raised: the
Medellín/Bogotá conflict between the brand material and the tenant configuration, the public naming of
the five classes against the four movements, whether Unlimited Pauses stacks with Membership and
whether a Pausa spends the one-class-a-day limit, the guest allowance per member, the off-peak hours a
space rental may use and whether it may displace a class, the rental deposit and cancellation terms,
the payment method and withholding for teacher payroll, who posts on social and approves price claims,
third-party use of the wordmark, and who owns the rights to media shot in the studio. Each one is
flagged **once**, in the chapter that owns it; other chapters cross-reference rather than repeat, so
the list has no duplicates.

## Logic and integrations
- Extracted at build/run time from the markdown; editing a chapter updates the list, no code change.
- Same list feeds ROADMAP.md "Open decisions for the owner".
- Integrations: none

## Real vs mock
- Real: the page renders from the data layer (`useData()` / `useTable`) over the tables above; every write goes through the `DataProvider` so the Supabase provider replaces the mock unchanged.
- Mock / pending: no external integrations; data is the browser-local `MockProvider` seed.
