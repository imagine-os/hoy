# S-01 — Role home & demo switcher

**Route** `/#/staff` · **Roles** super_admin, coordinator, front_desk, teacher, finance, customer · **Surface** staff · **Spec** `spec.code === 'S-01'` (see `/#/dev/specs`)

## Purpose
One app, six doors. The demo switcher in the toolbar swaps role so the whole system can be reviewed as any user. For the desk it is also the morning glance: what is next, who arrived, what was paid — and, since 0.8.0, who wrote to the studio and has not been answered (unread tile, recent conversations, the inbox action → S-06).

## Sections (layout order — `useLayout(spec)`)
1. `RoleBadge + KPIRow` — arrivals · open shifts · payments (or waitlist) · **Mensajes sin leer** (`StatTile`, hint "en N conversaciones", for roles with `members.read`; the waitlist tile stays for the others)
2. `NextClassCard`
3. `TodayList`
4. `QuickActions` — check-in · register · rooms (S-05) · **Abrir bandeja de mensajes** (S-06) · CRM · tables, each gated by its permission
5. `RecentMessages` — "Mensajes recientes": `ConversationList compact limit={5}` (unread first, avatar, last message, relative time, unread count) with "Ver todo" → `/staff/inbox`; each row → `/staff/inbox/:id`
6. `AuditNotice (recent activity)`

## Logic and integrations
- Teachers hold both a teacher and a student context and can switch freely.
- Permissions are additive per role; Super admin implies all.
- Every staff action is written to the audit log with actor, target and timestamp.
- Demo switching exists in the prototype only, never in production.
- Unread messages are the team's count, not the user's: a row is unread until someone opens its thread in S-06 or M-06 (`markConversationRead`).
- Integrations: Supabase Auth

## States
- No role assigned: read-only student view
- Context switch: full re-route

## Real vs mock
- Real: the page renders from the data layer (`useData()` / `useTable`) over the tables above; every write goes through the `DataProvider` so the Supabase provider replaces the mock unchanged.
- Mock / pending: Supabase Auth are simulated or deferred; data is the browser-local `MockProvider` seed.
- Note: Live: subscribes to bookings, class_sessions and message_log through useTable.
- Mock / pending: the unread messages are seed rows (six across five people at first load) until the WhatsApp / email webhooks write inbound rows.
