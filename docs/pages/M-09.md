## Logic and integrations
- Revenue = approved payments in the selected range grouped by plan and by method.
- Refund flips payments.status to refunded and writes audit_log payment.refund; the credit return is a follow-up.
- E-invoicing requires a DIAN resolution in M-08; until then the CUFE column shows —.
- Integrations: Wompi, DIAN e-invoicing

## States
- Loading
- Empty range
- No refund permission: action hidden
- E-invoicing off: notice

## Real vs mock
- Real: the page renders from the data layer (`useData()` / `useTable`) over the tables above; every write goes through the `DataProvider` so the Supabase provider replaces the mock unchanged.
- Mock / pending: Wompi, DIAN e-invoicing are simulated or deferred; data is the browser-local `MockProvider` seed.
- Note: Placeholder payouts until the Wompi integration lands.
