# W-11 · Próximamente / Coming soon

Routes: `/` and `/#/coming-soon` · Surface: public · Roles: everyone

The public root pre-launch surface, also accessible from its own hub card at /hub. It is separate from the complete `/site` experience and its version selector. The existing brand wordmark accompanies the approved real studio photograph under red lighting, with mirrors and prepared mats. The packaged 480/960/1600 px WebP variants serve this full-viewport background; the original photo is unchanged.

## Content and behavior

- Spanish by default; ES/EN uses the shared language control
- “Muévete. Respira. Vive el presente.” comes from the approved brand content
- The opening status is simply “Próximamente / Coming soon”; no date or countdown is claimed
- The main CTA opens the already-confirmed studio Instagram profile in a new tab, not a popup
- Top-right Login / Entrar opens sign-in (`#/auth/sign-in`), which also creates an account (owner, 30 Oct 2026; it was a "coming soon" dialog before). In the production build that is Clerk's email code or Google
- No contact form, email collection, mock signup or payment action
- The studio image is static in all motion and data-saving preferences; no concept video or playback control remains
- The standalone landing remains independent from website edition selection
